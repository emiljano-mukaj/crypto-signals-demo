How this score was built

Four standard indicators, equal weight, scored on a fixed scale.

The model, in the open

Nothing hidden: every point in the score comes from one of these four readings on the last 120 daily candles.

±2

EMA trend

Where the 20-period average sits relative to the 50-period average, as a percentage of price.

±2

RSI (14)

Momentum bands. Oversold below 30 adds points, overbought above 70 subtracts them.

±2

MACD histogram

The 12/26 EMA difference minus its 9-period signal, normalised by price so pairs are comparable.

±1

On-balance volume

Whether volume is accumulating on up days or down days across the last 14 sessions.
